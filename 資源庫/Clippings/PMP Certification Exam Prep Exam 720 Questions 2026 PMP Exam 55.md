Question 5:

Case Study 1: Q5

A project manager is overseeing the development of a new accounting software platform for a mid-sized financial services firm. During planning, the project team defined the full scope of the system including general ledger, accounts payable, accounts receivable, and financial reporting modules. The scope was documented and approved by the project sponsor before development began.

Following a competitive bidding process, the project team selected an external software development vendor to build the platform. The scope and deliverables were fully defined prior to vendor selection and the contract value was agreed upon before work began. The contract specified that all deliverables must meet industry standard quality benchmarks for accounting software.

During the development period, the firm acquired two smaller companies, significantly increasing the volume of daily transactions the platform would need to process at launch.

Upon completion, the vendor delivered the platform. During the project manager's formal review, the platform performed within the benchmarks specified in the contract but struggled under the transaction volumes now required by the expanded organization. The vendor maintains that the deliverables meet the terms of the contract.

The project sponsor, aware of the cost and time required to address the performance gap, has suggested that the firm accept the platform as delivered and address the performance issues post-launch.

\---

Question 5: What could the project manager have done during planning to prevent the situation described in the case study?  
問題五：專案經理在規劃期間可以採取哪些措施來避免案例研究中描述的情況？

Conducted a more thorough vendor evaluation process to select a vendor with greater technical capability.  
進行更全面的供應商評估流程，以選擇技術能力更強的供應商。

Your answer is incorrect 你的回答不正確

Established a vendor monitoring plan to track progress and quality throughout the development period.  
建立了供應商監控計畫，追蹤整個開發期間的進度與品質。

Correct answer 正確答案

Defined specific measurable performance thresholds in the contract rather than referencing industry standard benchmarks.  
在合約中明確定義具體可衡量的績效門檻，而非參考產業標準基準。

Required the vendor to submit regular progress reports to identify performance issues early.  
要求供應商定期提交進度報告，以及早發現績效問題。
