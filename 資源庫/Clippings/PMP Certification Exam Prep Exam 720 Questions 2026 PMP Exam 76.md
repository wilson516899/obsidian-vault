Question 67:

While leading a project at an innovative tech startup specializing in advanced cloud-based training for agile methodologies and DevOps practices, the project manager faces a setback. The final product, designed to accommodate diverse learning methods, was deployed without comprehensive quality checks by the quality assurance (QA) team, leading to negative feedback from users due to several unnoticed issues.  
在領導一家專注於敏捷方法論與 DevOps 進階雲端訓練的創新科技新創公司專案時，專案經理遇到挫折。最終產品設計以適應多元學習方法，品質保證（QA）團隊未進行全面品質檢查，導致用戶因多處未被注意的問題而給予負面反饋。

What issue is the project manager primarily dealing with?  
專案經理主要處理什麼問題？

Insufficient user acceptance testing  
使用者驗收測試不足

Correct answer 正確答案

Release of a defective product  
有缺陷產品的釋出

Incomplete project deliverables  
未完成的專案交付成果

Your answer is incorrect 你的回答不正確

Failure in meeting user experience benchmarks  
未能達到使用者體驗基準
