Question 113:問題113：

For an upcoming infrastructure upgrade project, the project manager learns that the preferred local technology vendors are temporarily closed due to unforeseen economic constraints. Viable alternatives are accessible from international markets.  
針對即將進行的基礎設施升級專案，專案經理得知當地首選的技術供應商因突發經濟限制暫時關閉。可行的替代方案可從國際市場取得。

What action should the project manager undertake to ensure the project remains on schedule?  
專案經理應採取什麼行動以確保專案按時進行？

Your answer is incorrect 你的回答不正確

Conduct a risk analysis to evaluate the impact of using non-local vendors on project timelines and costs.  
進行風險分析，評估使用非本地供應商對專案時程與成本的影響。

Propose a budget increase to the project steering committee to cover potential higher costs from international sourcing.  
向專案指導委員會提議增加預算，以涵蓋國際採購可能增加的成本。

Correct answer 正確答案

Shift to a digital procurement process, allowing for the selection and engagement of international vendors via online platforms.  
轉向數位採購流程，允許透過線上平台選擇並接觸國際供應商。

Request a project delay from the project governance board until local vendors are operational again.  
請向專案治理委員會申請延後專案，直到當地廠商恢復營運。
