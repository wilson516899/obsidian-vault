Question 97:問題 97：

A project manager is facing challenges in a construction project because stakeholders frequently bypass the project manager to communicate directly with architects and engineers. This direct interaction is disrupting project workflows and resource management.  
專案經理在施工專案中面臨挑戰，因為利害關係人經常繞過專案經理，直接與建築師和工程師溝通。這種直接互動正在干擾專案工作流程與資源管理。

What should the project manager do to address this issue effectively?  
專案經理應該如何有效處理這個問題？

Your answer is incorrect 你的回答不正確

Develop a new stakeholder engagement strategy to better define the roles and interaction guidelines.  
制定新的利害關係人參與策略，以更明確地定義角色與互動指引。

Schedule regular briefings with senior management to discuss and streamline communication norms.  
定期與高層管理人員簡報，討論並簡化溝通規範。

Correct answer 正確答案

Enforce the communications management plan by reiterating its importance to all project stakeholders.  
透過向所有專案利害關係人重申溝通管理計畫的重要性，來執行其重要性。

Facilitate a series of integration meetings to ensure all parties understand the communication hierarchy.  
安排一系列整合會議，確保各方了解溝通層級。
