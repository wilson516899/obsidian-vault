Question 182:問題 182：

The risk associated with performance specifications is usually the responsibility of the:  
與性能規格相關的風險，通常由：

Project manager 項目經理

Customer 客戶

Your answer is incorrect 您的回答不正確。

Project sponsor 項目發起人

Correct answer 正確答案

Seller 賣家

When contracts use performance specifications (also called functional specifications), the seller assumes the risk of determining how to meet the specified performance requirements. The buyer defines what results are required (output, capacity, efficiency, reliability) but not how to achieve them, giving the seller flexibility in design and implementation approaches. If the seller's chosen approach fails to meet performance specifications, the seller bears responsibility for redesign, rework, or replacement at their own cost. This contrasts with design specifications where the buyer specifies exactly how work should be performed, placing more risk on the buyer if the prescribed approach doesn't achieve desired results. Performance specifications transfer technical risk to sellers who have specialized expertise.  
當合同中使用性能規格（也稱為功能規格）時，賣方承擔確定如何滿足指定性能要求的風險。買方定義所需的成果（如輸出、容量、效率、可靠性），但未指定如何實現這些成果，從而為賣方在設計和實施方法上提供了靈活性。如果賣方選擇的方法未能滿足性能規格，則賣方負責重新設計、修改或更換，並承擔相關費用。這與設計規格不同，設計規格要求買方明確指定工作方式，如果所選方法未能達到預期效果，則買方承擔更多風險。性能規格將技術風險轉移給具備專業知識的賣方。

**Why the other options are not correct:  
其他選項不正確的原因：**

The project manager coordinates and oversees the contract but doesn't assume the technical risk of meeting performance specifications, which lies with the party responsible for delivery.

The customer (buyer) establishes what performance is required but transfers the risk of how to achieve it to the seller, retaining only the risk of defining appropriate performance targets.

The project sponsor provides funding and strategic direction but doesn't assume technical responsibility for the seller's ability to meet contractually specified performance levels.
