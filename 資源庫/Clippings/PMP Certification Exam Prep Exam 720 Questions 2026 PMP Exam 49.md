Question 173:問題 173：

Which of the following is NOT true about a cost reimbursable contract?  
以下哪一項是不符合成本報銷合同的？

The buyer reimburses the seller for actual costs incurred plus a fee  
買方將實際發生的費用以及一定的費用，報銷給賣方。

The buyer assumes most of the cost risk in the contract  
合同中，買方承擔大部分的風險。

Correct answer 正確答案

The seller has maximum incentive to control costs and maximize efficiency  
賣家有最大的動力來控制成本並提高效率。

Your answer is incorrect 您的回答不正確。

This contract type is appropriate when scope is uncertain or difficult to define  
這種合同類型適用於範圍不明或難以界定的情況。
