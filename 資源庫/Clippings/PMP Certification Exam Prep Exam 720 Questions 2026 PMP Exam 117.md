Question 138:

For a high-stakes innovation project, a project manager is leading a group of talented individuals who are eager but have never worked together. The project manager planned an external retreat to discuss project goals and team dynamics, which was subsequently denied funding by the project sponsor, citing budgetary restraints. Understanding the importance of team cohesion, the project manager is determined to find an alternative.  
對於一個高風險的創新專案，專案經理帶領一群充滿熱忱但從未合作過的優秀人才。專案經理計劃舉辦外部靜修會，討論專案目標與團隊動態，但因預算限制，計畫贊助方拒絕資助。專案經理理解團隊凝聚力的重要性，決心尋找替代方案。

What action should the project manager take to foster team unity and alignment without additional expenses?  
專案經理應該採取什麼行動，才能促進團隊團結與協調，同時避免額外費用？

Propose a virtual team-building exercise that leverages digital collaboration tools  
提出一項結合數位協作工具的虛擬團隊建設演習

Accept the sponsor's decision and focus solely on project deliverables to maximize efficiency  
接受贊助人的決定，專注於專案交付物以最大化效率

Your answer is incorrect 你的回答不正確

Invite team members to contribute ideas for low-cost or no-cost team-building activities  
邀請團隊成員提出低成本或免費團隊建設活動的點子

Correct answer 正確答案

Arrange a cost-effective team-building event within the office or company facilities  
在辦公室或公司設施內安排具成本效益的團隊建設活動
