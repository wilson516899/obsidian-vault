Question 72:

A project manager observes that the project is falling behind schedule because key decisions are delayed by conflicting perspectives between the organization's senior management and external stakeholders.

What should the project manager do?  
專案經理應該怎麼做？

Review the stakeholder register and ensure communications follow the communications management plan  
審查利害關係人登記冊，並確保溝通符合溝通管理計畫

Isolate the project team from external factors like senior management and stakeholders to enable faster decisions  
將專案團隊與高層管理及利害關係人等外部因素隔離，以促進更快決策

Your answer is incorrect 你的回答不正確

Review the remaining scope and rebaseline the schedule to account for decision-making delays  
檢視剩餘範圍並重新設定時程基數，以因應決策延遲

Correct answer 正確答案

Review the adequacy of project governance and ensure appropriate structure is in place  
檢視專案治理的充分性，並確保有適當的架構
