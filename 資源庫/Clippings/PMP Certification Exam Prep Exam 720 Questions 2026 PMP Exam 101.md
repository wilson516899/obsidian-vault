Question 104:

In the midst of a critical infrastructure project, the project manager realizes that the contract duration for a key vendor covers only half the project timeline, necessitating an unexpected second tender process for the latter half.  
在關鍵基礎設施專案進行中，專案經理發現關鍵供應商的合約期限僅涵蓋專案時程的一半，因此後半段需要突如其來的第二次招標程序。

To ensure continuous support, what should have been the initial approach?  
為了確保持續的支持，最初應該採取什麼方法？

Correct answer 正確答案

Craft a comprehensive procurement plan covering the entire project duration for client endorsement  
制定涵蓋整個專案期間的全面採購計畫，以取得客戶背書

Directly involve the client in drafting the contract to ensure their expectations are fully met  
直接讓客戶參與合約起草，確保他們的期望能被充分滿足

Initiate a flexible contract with the option to extend based on project needs and client feedback  
啟動彈性合約，並可根據專案需求及客戶回饋選擇延長

Your answer is incorrect 你的回答不正確

Secure an agreement for provisional services extension under the same terms, pending project evaluation  
在專案評估期間，依照相同條款取得臨時服務延長協議
