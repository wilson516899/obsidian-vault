Question 39:問題39：

Following the successful delivery of a project under a Time and Materials (T&M) contract, the client identifies defects and insists on complimentary fixes, a request met with a proposal for additional charges by the project manager, leading to a dispute.  
在以時間與材料（T&M）合約成功交付專案後，客戶發現缺陷並堅持提供免費修復，專案經理提出額外收費建議，導致爭議。

What preventive measure could the project manager have implemented to avoid this disagreement?  
專案經理可以採取什麼預防措施來避免這種分歧？

Implemented a training program for the client's team on post-delivery maintenance and support  
為客戶團隊實施交付後維護與支援的培訓計畫

Your answer is incorrect 你的回答不正確

Conducted a thorough walkthrough of the project’s quality control measures with the client before delivery  
在交付前，與客戶進行了專案品質控管措施的全面巡查

Proposed a contract modification to include a post-delivery defect resolution period at no additional cost  
提議合約修改，納入交付後缺陷解決期且無額外費用

Correct answer 正確答案

Outlined a post-delivery support plan detailing how product maintenance would be handled
