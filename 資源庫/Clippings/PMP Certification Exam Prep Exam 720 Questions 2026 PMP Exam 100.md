Question 103:問題103：

While initiating a product enhancement project, a project manager discovers historical records indicating that a comparable project led to customer complaints about degraded user experience relative to the prior version.  
在啟動產品增強專案時，專案經理發現歷史紀錄顯示，類似專案曾引發客戶抱怨使用者體驗相較於前版本下降。

What action best prevents this outcome?  
什麼行動能最好地防止這種結果？

Your answer is incorrect 你的回答不正確

Engage external users to conduct product acceptance testing  
邀請外部使用者進行產品驗收測試

Correct answer 正確答案

Establish a user focus group as high-influence external stakeholders  
建立一個由外部利害關係人組成的用戶焦點團體

Contract a consultant to conduct benefit analysis for the project charter  
聘請顧問為專案章程進行效益分析

Document a high-level risk and plan mitigation following WBS completion  
記錄高階風險並規劃 WBS 完成後的緩解措施
