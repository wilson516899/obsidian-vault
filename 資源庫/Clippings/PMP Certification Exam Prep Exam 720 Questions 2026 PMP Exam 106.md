Question 109:

In the midst of a renewable energy project, a team member notices a significant risk related to equipment procurement that could potentially derail the project timeline. The concern is promptly communicated to the project sponsor, who then requests the project manager for a detailed assessment and action plan. Following discussions with the sponsor, the project manager resolves to enact preventive measures.  
在一個再生能源專案進行中，一名團隊成員注意到一項與設備採購相關的重大風險，這可能會使專案時程偏離軌道。該關切會迅速傳達給專案贊助人，主辦方隨後要求專案經理提供詳細評估與行動計畫。在與贊助商討論後，專案經理決定採取預防措施。

Which document should the project manager consult with the team to address this procurement risk appropriately?  
專案經理應該與團隊協商哪份文件，以適當處理這項採購風險？

Supplier coordination strategy  
供應商協調策略

Your answer is incorrect 你的回答不正確

Stakeholder engagement strategy  
利害關係人參與策略

Correct answer 正確答案

廠商談判策略
