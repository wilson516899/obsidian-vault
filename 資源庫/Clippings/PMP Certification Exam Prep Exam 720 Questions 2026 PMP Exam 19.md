Question 36:

A project manager is developing the cost baseline for a construction project when the risk management team identifies several known risks that could impact project costs, including potential weather delays and material price fluctuations. The project manager needs to allocate funds to address these identified risks if they occur.  
項目經理正在為一項建築項目制定成本基準，但風險管理團隊發現了一些已知的風險，這些風險可能會影響項目成本，包括潛在的天氣延誤和材料價格波動。因此，項目經理需要預留資金，以應對這些已識別的風險。

What should the project manager include in the cost baseline?  
項目經理在制定成本基準時，應該包含哪些內容？

Management reserves 管理層保留

Cost variance 成本差異

Correct answer 正確答案

Contingency reserves 備用資金
