Question 75:

Towards the end of a large-scale infrastructure development project, the project manager detects a slowdown in progress with a significant number of tasks pending in the project's quality assurance stage. The project is managed using a combination of traditional and agile methodologies, with limited time available before the project deadline.  
在大型基礎建設開發專案接近尾聲時，專案經理發現進度放緩，且在品質保證階段有大量待完成的任務。專案以傳統與敏捷方法結合管理，且在截止日前可節省時間。

What action should the project manager take?  
專案經理應該採取什麼行動？

Initiate an intensive quality assurance training session for the project team  
為專案團隊啟動密集的品質保證培訓課程

Correct answer 正確答案

Bring in an external consultant with expertise in quality assurance  
請聘請具備品質保證專業知識的外部顧問

Your answer is incorrect 你的回答不正確

在專案時程中特別分配額外時間給品質保證任務
