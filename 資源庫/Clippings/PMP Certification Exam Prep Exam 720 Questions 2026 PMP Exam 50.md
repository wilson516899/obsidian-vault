Question 174:

A project manager has negotiated a Cost Plus Incentive Fee (CPIF) contract with the following terms:  
項目經理已與承包商達成一項“成本加成激勵費”（CPIF）合同，該合同的條款如下：

- Target Cost: $150,000 目標成本：15 萬美元
- Target Fee: $15,000
- Actual Cost: $163,000 實際成本：163,000 美元
- Share Ratio: 70/30 (buyer/seller)

What are the final fee and final price for this contract?  
這份合同的最終費用和最終價格分別是多少？

$11,100 and $174,100 11,100 美元和 174,100 美元
