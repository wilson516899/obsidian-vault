Question 102:

In planning a software development project that requires periodic updates to cloud-based services from multiple vendors, the project manager is optimistic about securing competitive rates due to high vendor interest.  
在規劃一個需要多家廠商定期更新雲端服務的軟體開發專案時，專案經理對於因廠商高度興趣而取得具競爭力的價格持樂觀態度。

What should the project manager do first to ensure favorable terms and value in future agreements?  
專案經理應該首先做什麼，才能確保未來協議中有利的條款與價值？

Your answer is incorrect 你的回答不正確

Consult with senior IT management to leverage historical vendor performance and pricing data  
諮詢高階 IT 管理，利用歷史供應商績效與定價數據

Initiate discussions with potential vendors to gauge their service level agreements and pricing models  
與潛在供應商展開討論，評估其服務水準協議與定價模式

Correct answer 正確答案

Review the organization's procurement policies and any legal requirements that may influence the agreements  
檢視組織的採購政策及可能影響協議的法律要求

Draft a detailed requirements document that outlines the expected frequency and scope of updates from vendors  
擬定一份詳細的需求文件，說明供應商更新的頻率與範圍
