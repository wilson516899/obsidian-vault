Question 76:問題76：

Upon discovering a significant quality issue with a key supplier's deliverables, the project manager needs to address the problem to maintain project standards.  
當發現關鍵供應商交付物有重大品質問題時，專案經理需要處理該問題以維持專案標準。

What should be the project manager's initial action?  
專案經理應該採取什麼初步行動？

Schedule an urgent project team meeting to brainstorm potential solutions to the quality issue.  
安排緊急專案團隊會議，集思廣益討論品質問題的潛在解決方案。

Your answer is incorrect 你的回答不正確

Request an immediate, comprehensive quality audit on all supplier deliverables to identify the scope of the problem.  
立即要求對所有供應商交付物進行全面且完整的品質稽核，以釐清問題範圍。

Correct answer 正確答案

Refer to the project's risk management plan for predetermined mitigation actions.  
請參閱專案的風險管理計畫，以了解預先設定的緩解措施。

Draft an urgent communication to the supplier demanding an explanation and proposed rectification plan.  
起草一份緊急通知給供應商，要求說明並提出修正計畫。
