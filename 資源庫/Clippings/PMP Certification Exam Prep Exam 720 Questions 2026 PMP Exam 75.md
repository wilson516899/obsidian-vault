Question 64:問題64：

As the project manager overseeing the rollout of a new IT system, it's discovered halfway through the fiscal year that several critical software enhancements, funded by this year's budget, will not be completed on schedule. These enhancements were initiated based on mid-year change requests and are crucial for the project's success.  
作為負責新 IT 系統部署的專案經理，我們發現今年預算資助的多項關鍵軟體升級無法如期完成。這些改進是根據年中變更請求啟動的，對專案成功至關重要。

What is the most effective course of action?  
最有效的行動方案是什麼？

Your answer is incorrect 你的回答不正確

Initiate a review of the project scope to identify potential areas for schedule acceleration  
啟動專案範圍審查，以找出可加速進度的潛在領域

Collaborate with the project team to re-prioritize the project's deliverables to focus on critical enhancements  
與專案團隊合作，重新調整專案交付物的優先順序，聚焦於關鍵的改進

Engage with stakeholders to adjust expectations and secure support for extended timelines  
與利害關係人互動，調整期望並爭取延長時程的支持

Correct answer 正確答案

Communicate the progress and adjusted timelines for these enhancements to the finance department  
向財務部門溝通這些改進的進展與調整後的時間表
