Question 91:

In navigating the complexities of an agile project, the project manager seeks methods to significantly enhance team engagement and maintain high motivation levels.

What action should the project manager consider as a primary strategy?  
專案經理應該考慮什麼行動作為主要策略？

Shift team members between various project tasks to prevent monotony and stimulate interest  
在不同專案任務間輪換團隊成員，避免單調並激發興趣

Your answer is incorrect 你的回答不正確

Articulate project goals and roles distinctly to ensure team members have clarity on their contributions  
明確說明專案目標與角色，確保團隊成員清楚了解自己的貢獻

Correct answer 正確答案

Establish a recognition program that celebrates outstanding contributions identified during sprint retrospectives  
建立一個表彰計畫，表彰在短跑回顧中被識別出的傑出貢獻

Arrange for collaborative team-building activities to deepen the connection between team members and the project objectives  
安排協作團隊建設活動，以深化團隊成員與專案目標之間的連結
