Question 29:

Upon a recent change in project leadership, the incoming product owner's unfamiliarity with the project specifics has led to the team undervaluing their input, often deferring instead to the project manager's direction.  
近期專案領導層變動後，新任產品負責人對專案細節不熟悉，導致團隊低估了他們的意見，常常將意見交由專案經理指揮。

What action should the project manager take to integrate the new product owner effectively?  
專案經理應該採取什麼行動，才能有效整合新產品負責人？

Correct answer 正確答案

Collaborate with the PMO to develop a comprehensive orientation strategy for the new product owner  
與 PMO 合作，為新產品負責人制定全面的導向策略

Dedicate sessions to brief the new product owner on critical project details and product functionalities  
設立專門會議，向新任產品負責人簡報關鍵專案細節與產品功能

Temporarily adopt the product owner's responsibilities until they are up to speed with the project's demands  
暫時接手產品負責人的職責，直到他們熟悉專案需求

Your answer is incorrect 你的回答不正確

Facilitate a team integration exercise emphasizing the importance of valuing all team members' contributions  
主持團隊整合演練，強調重視所有成員貢獻的重要性
