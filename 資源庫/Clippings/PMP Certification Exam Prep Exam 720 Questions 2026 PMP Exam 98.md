In the midst of organizing a large-scale international conference, a project manager notices that the delivery of custom event materials, which are vital for the conference's branding, is significantly delayed. This delay poses a risk to the event's timeline and potentially compromises marketing efforts. It was later found that the responsible team member had attempted to contact the supplier via email with no success.  
在籌辦一場大型國際會議期間，一位專案經理注意到對會議品牌至關重要的客製化活動材料交付大幅延遲。這種延遲對活動時程構成風險，並可能影響行銷成效。後來發現負責的團隊成員曾嘗試透過電子郵件聯繫供應商，但未成功。
