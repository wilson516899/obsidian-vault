Question 74:問題 74：

Which one of the following is NOT an outcome when a project is initiated properly?  
以下哪一項不是一個經過良好規劃的項目應該產生的結果？

The project is formally authorized and linked to organizational strategy  
該項目已正式獲得批准，並與組織戰略相協調。

Correct answer 正確答案

Detailed work breakdown structure and activity schedules are created  
詳細的工作分解結構和活動時間表將被創建。

Key stakeholders are identified and their influence is understood  
確定關鍵利益相關者，並瞭解他們的影響

Your answer is incorrect 您的回答不正確。

The project manager is assigned and given authority over resources  
項目經理負責，並擁有資源的管理權限。
