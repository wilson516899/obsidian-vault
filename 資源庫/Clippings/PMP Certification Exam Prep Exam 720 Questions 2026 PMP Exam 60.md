Question 31:

The project team has voiced concerns about the scrum master's effectiveness, citing a failure to address key obstacles that hindered the last sprint's progress.

What is the most appropriate response from the project manager to address these concerns?  
專案經理對於這些疑慮，最合適的回應是什麼？

Introduce an additional team member focused solely on removing the identified impediments  
增設一名專注於消除已識別障礙的團隊成員

Your answer is incorrect 你的回答不正確

Clarify the criteria for identifying and addressing impediments with the scrum master's intervention  
釐清識別與處理障礙的標準，並透過 Scrum Master 的介入

Correct answer 正確答案

Facilitate a session for the team to categorize and prioritize impediments for immediate resolution  
安排一場會議，讓團隊分類並優先排序障礙，以便立即解決

Inform the team that they should independently find solutions to overcome project challenges without relying on the scrum master  
告知團隊應自行尋找解決方案，克服專案挑戰，而不應依賴 Scrum Master
