Question 24:問題 24：

A project manager is overseeing a hospital equipment installation project that is currently two weeks behind schedule. The contract includes significant financial penalties for late completion, and the client has made it clear they cannot accept any delays beyond the current two-week variance. The project manager needs to bring the project back on schedule.  
項目經理正在負責一個醫院設備安裝項目，該項目目前已經比原計劃晚了兩個星期。合同規定，如果延期完成，將面臨嚴重的經濟處罰，而客戶明確表示，任何超過當前兩個週期的延期都無法接受。因此，項目經理需要儘快將項目恢復到原計劃的時間表。

What should the project manager do to address this schedule delay?  
項目經理應該如何應對這一進度延誤？

Fast-track the remaining activities by performing tasks in parallel that were planned sequentially  
通過並行執行原計劃的各項任務，從而加快剩餘活動的進度。

Negotiate with the client for a revised completion date to avoid the financial penalties  
與客戶協商，以避免罰款，確定新的項目完成日期。

Correct answer 正確答案

Crash the project 項目失敗

Your answer is incorrect 您的回答不正確。

Remove lower-priority deliverables from the project scope to reduce the overall timeline  
從項目範圍中移除低優先級的任務，以縮短整體時間表。
