## 🧭 PMBOK 8 分類架構（標註依據）

### 6 大原則 Principles

| 原則 | 中譯 |
|---|---|
| Adopt a Holistic View | 採取整體視角 |
| Focus on Value | 聚焦價值 |
| Embed Quality | 內建品質 |
| Be an Accountable Leader | 當負責的領導者 |
| Integrate Sustainability | 整合永續 |
| Build an Empowered Culture | 建立賦權文化 |

### 7 大績效領域 Performance Domains

| 領域 | 中譯 | 流程數 | 涵蓋主題 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Governance | 治理 | 9 | 整合、變更、品質保證、知識管理、啟動/收尾 |
| Scope | 範疇 | 6 | 需求、範疇定義與確認 |
| Schedule | 時程 | 3 | 排程規劃與控制 |
| Finance | 財務 | 4 | 成本估算、預算、財務控制 |
| Stakeholders | 關係人 | 7 | 辨識、參與、溝通 |
| **Resources** | **資源** | **5** | **團隊發展、衝突、領導、實體資源** |
| Risk | 風險 | 6 | 辨識、分析、回應、監控 |

### 5 個 Focus Areas × 40 流程

| Focus Area | 流程數 |
|---|---|
| Initiating 啟動 | 1 |
| Planning 規劃 | 20 |
| Executing 執行 | 8 |
| Monitoring and Controlling 監控 | 10 |
| Closing 收尾 | 1 |

<details><summary>40 流程完整清單</summary>

**Initiating（1）**
- Initiate Project or Phase〔Governance〕

**Planning（20）**
- Integrate and Align Project Plans、Plan Sourcing Strategy〔Governance〕
- Plan Scope Management、Elicit and Analyze Requirements、Define Scope、Develop Scope Structure〔Scope〕
- Plan Schedule Management、Develop Schedule〔Schedule〕
- Plan Financial Management、Estimate Costs、Develop Budget〔Finance〕
- Identify Stakeholders、Plan Stakeholder Engagement、Plan Communications Management〔Stakeholders〕
- Plan Resource Management、Estimate Resources〔Resources〕
- Plan Risk Management、Identify Risks、Perform Risk Analysis、Plan Risk Responses〔Risk〕

**Executing（8）**
- Manage Project Execution、Manage Quality Assurance、Manage Project Knowledge〔Governance〕
- Manage Stakeholder Engagement、Manage Communications〔Stakeholders〕
- Acquire Resources、**Lead the Team**〔Resources〕
- Implement Risk Responses〔Risk〕

**Monitoring and Controlling（10）**
- Monitor and Control Project Performance、Assess and Implement Changes〔Governance〕
- Monitor and Control Scope、Validate Scope〔Scope〕
- Monitor and Control Schedule〔Schedule〕
- Monitor and Control Finances〔Finance〕
- Monitor Stakeholder Engagement、Monitor Communications〔Stakeholders〕
- Monitor and Control Resourcing〔Resources〕
- Monitor Risks〔Risk〕

**Closing（1）**
- Close Project or Phase〔Governance〕

</details>
