Question 128:

A project team is pushing through to achieve an important deadline. However, due to the necessity of working overtime and the nature of their remote collaboration, team spirit has significantly dwindled. The lack of informal interaction has made it difficult for team members to bond or appreciate each other beyond their work contributions.

What measures should the project manager take to uplift the team's spirit and cohesion in this remote work setting?  
專案經理應該採取哪些措施來提升團隊在遠端工作的精神與凝聚力？

Implement a flexible work schedule to reduce burnout and acknowledge the team's hard work  
實施彈性的工作時間表以減少倦怠，並肯定團隊的辛勤付出

Your answer is incorrect 你的回答不正確

Create a rewards structure to enhance motivation and morale  
建立獎勵結構以提升動力與士氣

Correct answer 正確答案

Introduce virtual social events or casual catch-ups to encourage personal connections among team members  
引入虛擬社交活動或輕鬆聚會，促進團隊成員間的個人連結

Arrange for an online workshop focused on team building and stress management techniques  
安排一場專注於團隊建立與壓力管理技巧的線上工作坊
