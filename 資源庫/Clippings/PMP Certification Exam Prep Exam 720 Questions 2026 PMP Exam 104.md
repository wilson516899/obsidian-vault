Question 107:問題107：

During the launch phase of a new software development project, the project manager discovers that the only vendor capable of providing a necessary proprietary software component has quoted a price well beyond the allocated budget. The finance team is skeptical about the project's cost-effectiveness and potential return on investment due to this unexpected expense.  
在新軟體開發專案啟動階段，專案經理發現唯一能提供必要專有軟體元件的廠商報價遠超預算。財務團隊對專案的成本效益及投資回報持懷疑態度，因為這筆意外支出。

What should the project manager do to address the budget concern?  
專案經理應該如何處理預算問題？

Negotiate directly with the vendor for a discount based on long-term partnership potential  
直接與供應商協商，根據長期合作潛力爭取折扣

Correct answer 正確答案

Seek additional vendors, even if it means considering alternatives that require minor project adjustments  
尋求其他供應商，即使這意味著考慮需要小幅專案調整的替代方案

Your answer is incorrect 你的回答不正確

Request an increased budget from the project board, outlining the critical nature of the component  
向專案委員會申請增加預算，說明該組件的重要性

Proceed with the procurement, documenting the decision-making process for future reference  
繼續採購，並記錄決策過程以備未來參考
