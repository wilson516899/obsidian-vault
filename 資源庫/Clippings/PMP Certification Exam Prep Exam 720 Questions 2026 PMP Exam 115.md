Question 136:問題136：

In managing a high-stakes marketing campaign project, the project manager notes varied team dynamics: one key strategist rarely voices their insights in group settings, preferring solitary work, yet consistently delivers insightful analysis; another, the campaign manager, is highly communicative, often dominating the dialogue with creative ideas.  
在管理一個高風險的行銷活動專案時，專案經理注意到團隊動態多變：一位關鍵策略師很少在團體場合表達見解，偏好獨自工作，但卻始終提供深刻的分析;另一位是競選經理，溝通能力強，經常以創意點子主導對話。

How should the project manager optimize team interaction to ensure peak performance?  
專案經理應該如何優化團隊互動，以確保達到巔峰表現？

Your answer is incorrect 你的回答不正確

Implement a structured rotation where each team member leads a meeting, ensuring diverse leadership  
實施結構化輪流，由每位團隊成員主持會議，確保領導層的多元化

Recognize the existing team dynamics as productive, given the project's progress, and avoid unnecessary changes  
鑑於專案進展，認識到現有團隊動態是有生產力的，並避免不必要的改變

Correct answer 正確答案

Foster an environment where the quieter strategist is encouraged to share ideas more openly in team discussions  
營造一個環境，鼓勵較安靜的策略師在團隊討論中更坦率地分享想法

Designate the campaign manager as the primary point of contact for all project communications to streamline information flow  
指定活動經理為所有專案溝通的主要聯絡窗口，以簡化資訊流通
