Question 106:

Upon learning of a recent update in safety regulations that affects a current infrastructure development project, the project manager realizes that the construction methods and materials planned for use no longer comply. The project will need immediate adjustments to meet these updated standards.

What should the project manager do first?  
專案經理應該先做什麼？

Your answer is incorrect 你的回答不正確

Arrange for a comprehensive review of all project plans against the new safety standards  
安排對所有專案計畫依據新安全標準進行全面審查

Correct answer 正確答案

Notify the procurement team immediately to stop all current and future material orders until compliance is verified  
立即通知採購團隊停止所有現有及未來的物料訂單，直到合規確認

Schedule an urgent project review meeting to reassess project timelines and budgets in light of the new regulations  
安排緊急專案檢討會議，根據新法規重新評估專案時程與預算

Delegate the task of assessing the impact of the new regulations on the project materials to the safety officer  
將評估新規定對專案材料影響的任務委派給安全官員
