Question 192:問題 192：

A project selection committee is evaluating three potential projects and wants to use a financial metric that calculates the discount rate at which the net present value of all cash flows equals zero. This metric helps compare the profitability of different investment options.  
一個項目評估委員會正在評估三個潛在項目，並希望使用一種財務指標，該指標計算出所有現金流的淨現值等於零的折現率。 這種指標有助於比較不同投資方案的盈利能力。

What financial metric should the committee calculate?  
委員會應該計算哪些財務指標？

Your answer is incorrect 您的回答不正確。

Net present value (NPV) 淨現值 (NPV)

Payback period 回收期

Benefit-cost ratio (BCR) 成本效益比 (BCR)

Correct answer 正確答案

Internal rate of return (IRR)
