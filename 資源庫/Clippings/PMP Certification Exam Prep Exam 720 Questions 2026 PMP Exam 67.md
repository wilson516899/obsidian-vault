Question 49:

In managing Project Z, where all but one crucial task are ahead of the projected timeline, a request comes from upper management to shift some team members to assist with Project Y, urgently needing additional support to meet its looming deadlines.  
在管理專案 Z 時，除了一項重要任務外，其他任務都提前於預期時程，高層管理層提出要求調動部分團隊成員協助專案 Y，因為急需額外支援以趕上迫在眉睫的期限。

What is the most prudent action for the project manager to take?  
專案經理最審慎的行動是什麼？

Propose to management the reallocation of Project Z's entire team to prioritize Project Y's critical needs  
建議管理層重新分配 Project Z 整個團隊，以優先處理 Project Y 的關鍵需求

Suggest suspending Project Z's operations temporarily to focus all efforts on rescuing Project Y  
建議暫時暫停 Z 計畫的行動，將所有力量集中在拯救 Y 計畫上

Your answer is incorrect 你的回答不正確

Permit only those involved in non-essential tasks of Project Z to provide temporary support for Project Y  
僅允許參與 Z 計畫非必要任務者提供 Y 計畫的臨時支援

Correct answer 正確答案

Maintain all team members on Project Z to ensure the crucial task is completed as planned  
讓所有團隊成員持續參與 Project Z，確保關鍵任務如期完成
