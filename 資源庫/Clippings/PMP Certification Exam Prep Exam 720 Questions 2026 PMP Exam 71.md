Question 54:問題54：

In the planning phase of a large-scale software implementation project, the project manager decides to conduct an extensive analysis of potential risks associated with the project's timeline and budget. The goal is to understand the likelihood of project completion within the defined parameters and to identify which variables most significantly impact the project's outcomes.  
在大型軟體實施專案的規劃階段，專案經理會決定對專案時程與預算相關的潛在風險進行深入分析。目標是了解在定義參數內完成專案的可能性，並識別對專案成果影響最顯著的變數。

What technique should the project manager apply to evaluate and address these project risks?  
專案經理應該採用什麼技巧來評估並解決這些專案風險？

Develop a risk breakdown structure to categorize risks.  
建立風險分解結構來分類風險。

Your answer is incorrect 你的回答不正確

Conduct a sensitivity analysis to determine the impact of specific risks.  
進行敏感度分析以判斷特定風險的影響。

Utilize a cause-and-effect diagram to trace the origins of each risk.  
利用因果關係圖來追溯每個風險的起源。

Correct answer 正確答案

Perform a Monte Carlo simulation to forecast potential project outcomes.  
進行蒙地卡羅模擬以預測潛在專案成果。
