Question 51:問題51：

In the midst of implementing a new healthcare IT system, a project manager faces a challenge. The initial phase, involving the upgrade of existing hardware, is behind schedule, impacting the subsequent software implementation phase due to shared technical teams.  
在實施新的醫療資訊科技系統時，一位專案經理面臨一項挑戰。初期階段，即現有硬體升級，進度落後，因共用技術團隊，影響後續軟體實施階段。

What course of action should the project manager take?  
專案經理應該採取什麼行動方案？

Negotiate with other project managers for temporary resource sharing to expedite the hardware upgrade  
與其他專案經理協商暫時共享資源，以加快硬體升級

Correct answer 正確答案

Initiate a risk assessment to evaluate the impact of the delay on the overall project timeline and budget  
啟動風險評估，評估延遲對整體專案時程與預算的影響
