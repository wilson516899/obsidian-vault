A project manager has identified 45 risks during a brainstorming session with the team. Before developing detailed response strategies for all risks, the project manager wants to prioritize them by assessing each risk's probability of occurrence and potential impact on project objectives, then categorizing them as high, medium, or low priority.  
項目經理在團隊頭腦風暴中識別出 45 種潛在風險。在制定針對所有風險的詳細應對策略之前，項目經理希望首先對這些風險進行優先級排序，通過評估每種風險發生的可能性以及對項目目標的潛在影響，然後將它們分為高、中、低優先級。
