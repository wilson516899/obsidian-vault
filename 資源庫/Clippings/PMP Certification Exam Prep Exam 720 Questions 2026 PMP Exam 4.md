Question 10:

Case Study 2: Q4 案例研究 2：Q4

A project manager is leading the development of a new hospital management system for a regional healthcare network. During planning, the project team worked with key stakeholders, including hospital administrators, department heads, and clinical staff representatives, to document and agree upon the system's functional requirements. The agreed requirements were signed off by the project sponsor and the development team began work according to the established plan.  
項目經理正在領導一個區域醫療網絡的新醫院管理系統開發項目。在項目規劃階段，項目團隊與關鍵利益相關者（包括醫院管理者、部門負責人和臨床人員代表）合作，以記錄和確定系統的功能需求。這些已確定的需求得到了項目贊助人和開發團隊的批准，並按照已確定的計劃開始實施。

Throughout execution, several department heads have approached the project manager directly with requests for additional features. Eager to maintain positive relationships with senior stakeholders, the project manager has directed the team to incorporate these requests.  
在項目執行過程中，多個部門負責人直接向項目經理提出增加功能的請求。為了保持與高級利益相關者的良好關係，項目經理指示團隊採納這些要求。

As the project progressed, several development team members began proactively building features they anticipated stakeholders would request in the future. The team believed this approach would reduce rework and demonstrate responsiveness to stakeholder needs.  
隨著項目的推進，團隊成員開始主動開發他們預見到利益相關者未來可能需要的特性。團隊認為這種方法可以減少返工，並展示對利益相關者需求的響應。

As the project approaches its delivery date, the team is behind schedule and the budget has been exhausted earlier than projected. The project manager is struggling to explain the variance to the project sponsor.  
隨著項目臨近交付日期，團隊已經落後於計劃，並且預算也提前耗盡。項目經理正在努力向項目贊助人解釋這些差異。
