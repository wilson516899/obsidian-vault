Question 58:

As a critical deadline approaches, the project team is under pressure to work extra hours, causing visible signs of strain and decreased morale. The project manager seeks to uplift and energize the team to ensure project success.  
隨著關鍵截止近，專案團隊面臨加班壓力，導致明顯壓力與士氣低落。專案經理致力於提升並激勵團隊，以確保專案成功。

What strategy should the project manager employ to boost team morale and productivity?  
專案經理應該採用什麼策略來提升團隊士氣與生產力？

Introduce a temporary policy of remote work to offer flexibility  
引入臨時遠端工作政策以提供彈性

Your answer is incorrect 你的回答不正確

Organize a team-building event to foster unity and relieve stress  
舉辦團隊建設活動，促進團結並減輕壓力

Deploy a peer recognition program to highlight individual contributions  
部署同儕表彰計畫，以突顯個別貢獻

Correct answer 正確答案

Implement rewards and recognition for meeting key milestones during this intense period.  
在這段緊湊時期，對達成關鍵里程碑的獎勵與認可實施。
