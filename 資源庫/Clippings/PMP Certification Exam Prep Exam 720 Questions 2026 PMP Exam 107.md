Question 110:問題110：

In response to a newly implemented company-wide policy requiring dual sign-offs from both the finance and legal departments for any project procurements, a critical technology project faces potential delays in securing necessary software licenses and hardware. The project is already operating under strict deadlines.  
針對一項新實施的全公司政策，要求任何專案採購都必須由財務部門與法務部門雙重核准，一個關鍵的科技專案面臨取得必要軟體授權與硬體的潛在延遲。該項目已在嚴格的期限下運作。

What is the best approach for the project manager to ensure the project remains on schedule despite these new procurement challenges?  
專案經理在面對這些新的採購挑戰時，如何確保專案能按時進行，該採取什麼最佳方法？

Request a one-time policy waiver for the project, citing the urgent need for resources to avoid critical delays  
申請一次性免除專案保單，理由是急需資源以避免嚴重延誤

Correct answer 正確答案

Conduct an analysis of how the new sign-off procedure affects the project timeline and adjust the project plan to mitigate any impacts  
分析新的簽核程序如何影響專案時程，並調整專案計畫以減輕任何影響

Your answer is incorrect 你的回答不正確

Pre-arrange joint review sessions with finance and legal teams to expedite the approval process for project procurements  
事先安排與財務及法律團隊的聯合審查會議，以加速專案採購的核准流程

Strategize procurement activities to align with periods of lesser workload, minimizing the impact on the project's critical path  
策略性地安排採購活動，配合工作量較輕的時期，將對專案關鍵路徑的影響降到最低
