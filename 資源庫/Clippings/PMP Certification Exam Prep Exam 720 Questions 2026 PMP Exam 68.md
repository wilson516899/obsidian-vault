Question 50:問題50：

In a project designed to deploy new technology across several departments, a project manager notices a shift in stakeholder satisfaction levels. Despite the project adhering to its timeline and deliverables being met, feedback indicates a perception of diminishing returns in terms of value from the recent project increments.  
在一個旨在跨部門部署新技術的專案中，一位專案經理注意到利害關係人滿意度的變化。儘管專案遵守時程並完成交付成果，但反饋顯示近期專案增量的效益遞減。

What approach should the project manager take to address these concerns?  
專案經理應該採取什麼方法來解決這些問題？

Clarify to stakeholders that the timeline adherence and completed deliverables are the primary indicators of success, assuring them that the project is on the right track.  
向利害關係人說明，時程遵守與完成交付成果是成功的主要指標，確保專案正走在正確的軌道上。

Your answer is incorrect 你的回答不正確

Highlight to stakeholders the inherent fluctuations in value perception due to the varying nature of resources and expected benefits at different project stages.  
向利害關係人強調，由於不同專案階段資源性質與預期效益的不同，價值感知的固有波動。

Communicate to stakeholders that it is a common practice to tackle the most value-adding elements first, resulting in a perceived decrease in value as the project progresses.  
向利害關係人傳達，通常會先處理最具價值的元素，這會導致專案進展中價值的感知下降。

Correct answer 正確答案

Analyze the trends in value delivery with the team to identify any underlying issues, devise strategies for consistent value provision, and transparently relay these plans to stakeholders.  
與團隊分析價值交付趨勢，找出潛在問題，制定持續價值提供策略，並透明地向利害關係人傳達這些計畫。
