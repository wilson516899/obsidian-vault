Question 112:問題112：

Right at the outset of a technology upgrade project, it's discovered that a key software component will no longer be updated by the vendor. Anticipating such a scenario, the project manager had set aside emergency funds.  
在技術升級專案一開始，就會發現某個關鍵軟體元件將不再由廠商更新。預見此情形，專案經理預留了緊急資金。

What course of action should the project manager undertake?  
專案經理應該採取什麼行動方案？

Shift the project focus to an alternative technology that doesn't rely on the vendor's software  
將專案焦點轉向不依賴廠商軟體的替代技術

Your answer is incorrect 你的回答不正確

Explore the market for third-party support services for the discontinued software component  
探索已停產軟體元件的第三方支援服務市場

Initiate an internal development effort to create a substitute for the unsupported software  
啟動內部開發計畫，創造一個替代未受支援軟體的方案

Correct answer 正確答案

Alert the project sponsor to the issue and discuss the utilization of emergency funds  
通知專案贊助人此事並討論緊急資金的使用情況
