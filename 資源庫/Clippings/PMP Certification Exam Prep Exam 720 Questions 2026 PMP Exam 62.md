Question 36:

A business analyst is unable to progress on a task without feedback from a marketing consultant, who has been unresponsive due to time constraints.

What should the project manager do to ensure the project continues moving forward?  
專案經理應該做些什麼，才能確保專案持續推進？

Report the consultant's lack of availability to their supervisor to secure dedicated time for project consultations.  
向主管報告顧問無法配合，以便爭取專屬時間進行專案諮詢。

Record this as a new risk in the project risk log, assigning responsibility to the consultant to mitigate this risk by providing the needed input.  
將此列為專案風險日誌中的新風險，並指派顧問負責提供所需輸入來降低風險。

Correct answer 正確答案

Coordinate directly with the consultant to schedule a focused meeting between them and the business analyst to clarify project needs.  
直接與顧問協調，安排他們與業務分析師的專注會議，以釐清專案需求。

Your answer is incorrect 你的回答不正確

Instruct the business analyst to persist in attempts to connect with the consultant as it falls under their responsibility to obtain necessary information.  
指示業務分析師持續嘗試與顧問溝通，因為取得必要資訊屬於他們的責任。
