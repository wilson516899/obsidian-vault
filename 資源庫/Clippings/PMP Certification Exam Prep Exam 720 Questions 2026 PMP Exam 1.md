Question 4:問題 4：

Case Study 1: Q4 案例研究 1：Q4

A project manager has assembled a new team to develop a customer-facing web portal for a financial services company. The team members have not previously worked together and the project represents the first collaboration between several departments.  
項目經理組建了一個新的團隊，旨在為一家金融服務公司開發一個面向客戶的網頁門戶。該團隊成員此前從未一起工作過，而該項目是多個部門首次合作。

During the early weeks of the project, one team member has consistently delivered high-quality technical work and is considered the strongest individual contributor on the team. However, in team meetings, this team member has repeatedly interrupted colleagues and dismissed their ideas before they could be fully presented. Outside of meetings, the team member has declined requests for help from colleagues and has chosen to work through technical challenges independently rather than sharing approaches or solutions with the rest of the team.  
在項目初期，一名團隊成員一直表現出高質量的技術工作，被認為是團隊中最具影響力的個人。然而，在團隊會議中，該成員經常打斷其他成員的發言，並否定他們的想法，即使這些想法尚未完全表達。此外，該成員也拒絕同事的幫助請求，並選擇獨立解決技術難題，而不是與團隊其他成員分享經驗或解決方案。

As the project continues, other team members have become noticeably quieter during meetings, rarely volunteering ideas or raising concerns. Several team members have begun working in isolation on their assigned tasks rather than collaborating across workstreams. The project manager has observed these dynamics but has not yet taken action.  
隨著項目的進行，其他團隊成員在會議中變得更加沉默，很少主動提出建議或表達顧慮。 多個團隊成員開始獨立完成各自的任務，而不是進行跨團隊的協作。 項目經理已經觀察到這些動態，但尚未採取行動。

Question 4: The project manager has observed the team dynamics described in the case study and is ready to take action. What should the project manager do first?  
第四題：項目經理已經觀察到案例研究中描述的團隊動態，並且準備採取行動。那麼，項目經理應該首先做什麼？

Your answer is incorrect 您的回答不正確。

Raise the issue in the next team meeting to establish clear expectations for collaboration.  
在下一次團隊會議上提出這個問題，以便明確合作的期望。

Consult with the functional manager to determine the appropriate course of action.  
與功能經理溝通，以確定最合適的處理方式。

Document the behavior in the issue log and monitor the situation before taking action.  
在問題日誌中記錄行為，並在採取行動前，先進行觀察和監控。

Correct answer 正確答案

Meet privately with the team member to discuss the observed behavior.  
與團隊成員進行私下會談，以便討論觀察到的行為。
