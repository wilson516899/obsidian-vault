Question 13:問題13：

Just as the project manager begins planning for a high-profile technology upgrade, a key supplier informs them that a critical software component will be delayed due to unforeseen development challenges. This delay threatens to derail the project's tight schedule and could lead to significant disruptions.  
就在專案經理開始規劃一項高調的技術升級時，一位關鍵供應商通知他們，一個關鍵的軟體元件因突發開發挑戰將延遲交付。這種延遲可能擾亂專案緊湊的進度，並可能造成重大干擾。

What should the project manager do next to minimize the impact on the project schedule?  
專案經理接下來應該做什麼，才能將對專案進度的影響降到最低？

Your answer is incorrect 你的回答不正確

Initiate a risk analysis to explore the implications of the delay  
啟動風險分析，探討延遲的影響

Correct answer 正確答案

Document the delay in the issue log and assess impact mitigation strategies  
在問題日誌中記錄延遲情況並評估影響緩解策略

Follow the plan of action laid out in the risk management plan  
遵循風險管理計畫中所訂定的行動計畫
