You are managing a project that has the following activities:

![](https://img-c.udemycdn.com/redactor/raw/2018-04-26_06-13-41-c3ff3efdd51265eb724600051344e81f.png)

What is the critical path and project duration?  
關鍵路徑和項目持續時間分別是？

Correct answer 正確答案

Path B-D-E with 20 days total duration  
路徑 B-D-E，總時長 20 天

Path A-C-E with 16 days total duration  
A-C-E 路線，總共 16 天。

Your answer is incorrect 您的回答不正確。

Path B-D-F with 15 days total duration  
路徑 B-D-F，總時長 15 天

All three paths are critical with 20 days duration  
這三種路徑都需要 20 天的時間。

The critical path is the longest sequence of activities from project start to finish, determining the minimum project duration. Path B-D-E totals 20 days (7+6+7), Path A-C-E totals 16 days (8+1+7), and Path B-D-F totals 15 days (7+6+2). Since Path B-D-E is longest, it is the critical path and establishes the project duration at 20 days. Activities B, D, and E on the critical path have zero float, meaning any delay to these activities will delay the entire project. The other paths have float: A-C-E has 4 days of float and B-D-F has 5 days of float.  
關鍵路徑是項目從開始到結束的最長活動序列，它決定了項目的最短持續時間。路徑 B-D-E 共計 20 天（7+6+7），路徑 A-C-E 共計 16 天（8+1+7），路徑 B-D-F 共計 15 天（7+6+2）。由於路徑 B-D-E 是最長的，因此它是關鍵路徑，從而確定了項目的持續時間為 20 天。關鍵路徑上的活動 B、D 和 E 沒有浮動，這意味著對這些活動延誤，會導致整個項目延誤。其他路徑則有浮動：A-C-E 有 4 天的浮動，而 B-D-F 有 5 天的浮動。

**Why the other options are not correct:  
其他選項不正確的原因：**

Path A-C-E totals 16 days, making it 4 days shorter than Path B-D-E, so it is not the critical path and has 4 days of float available.  
路徑 A-C-E 總共需要 16 天，比路徑 B-D-E 縮短 4 天，因此它不是關鍵路徑，並且有 4 天的自由時間。

Path B-D-F totals 15 days, making it 5 days shorter than the critical path, so activities on this path have 5 days of scheduling flexibility.  
路徑 B-D-F 總共需要 15 天，比關鍵路徑縮短 5 天，因此在這個路徑上的活動可以有 5 天的靈活安排時間。

Only the longest path (B-D-E) is critical; the other paths have float and are not critical since they don't determine project duration.  
只有最長的路徑（B-D-E）至關重要；其他路徑可以靈活調整，因為它們不會影響項目的總時長。
