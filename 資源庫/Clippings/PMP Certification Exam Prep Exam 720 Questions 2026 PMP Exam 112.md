Question 129:問題129：

When the last stages of a project unexpectedly exceed their timeline, how might the application of agile principles offer improvement over traditional project management techniques?  
當專案的最後階段意外超過時程時，敏捷原則的應用如何能比傳統專案管理技術帶來改進？

Correct answer 正確答案

Emphasize iterative deliveries that provide tangible value at regular intervals  
強調定期提供實質價值的迭代交付

Facilitate continuous improvement discussions to identify and resolve bottlenecks  
促進持續改進討論，以識別並解決瓶頸

Apply velocity tracking to adjust future sprint planning based on current performance  
應用速度追蹤，根據當前表現調整未來衝刺計畫
