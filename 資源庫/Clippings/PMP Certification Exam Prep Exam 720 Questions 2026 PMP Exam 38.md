Question 126:問題 126：

A project manager is considering implementing just-in-time (JIT) management principles for a manufacturing project and wants to ensure the team understands this approach correctly.  
項目經理正在考慮將“準時生產”（JIT）的管理原則應用於一個製造項目，並希望確保團隊能夠正確理解這種方法。

All of the following statements about just-in-time management are FALSE EXCEPT:  
以下關於“準時管理”的陳述，除了以下哪一個是不正確的？

JIT increases inventory holding costs by requiring more frequent deliveries  
JIT (即時庫存) 會增加庫存持有成本，因為需要更頻繁的補貨。

JIT eliminates the need for reliable supplier relationships and coordination  
JIT 消除了依賴可靠的供應商關係和協調的需求。

Correct answer 正確答案

JIT delivers materials and components exactly when needed in production  
JIT (準時交貨) 確保在生產過程中，材料和零部件按時交付。

Your answer is incorrect 您的回答不正確。

JIT maintains large buffer inventories to prevent production disruptions  
JIT 採用大容量的庫存策略，以避免生產中斷。
