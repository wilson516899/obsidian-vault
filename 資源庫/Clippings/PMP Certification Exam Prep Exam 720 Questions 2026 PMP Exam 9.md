Question 23:

A project manager is analyzing quality issues reported during the testing phase of a software development project. Over the past month, the team has documented 47 different types of defects. The project manager wants to identify which defect types should be prioritized for corrective action to have the greatest impact on overall quality improvement.  
項目經理正在分析在軟件開發項目測試階段報告的質量問題。在過去的一個月裡，團隊記錄了 47 種不同的缺陷類型。項目經理希望確定哪些類型的缺陷應該優先處理，以便在整體質量改進方面產生最大的影響。

What analysis method should the project manager use?  
項目經理應該使用哪種分析方法？

Monte Carlo simulation to predict the probability of future defects  
利用蒙特卡羅模擬來預測未來缺陷的概率

Correct answer 正確答案

Pareto analysis to identify the defects causing the most significant problems  
利用帕累託分析，找出導致最嚴重問題的缺陷

Sensitivity analysis to determine which defects impact the schedule most  
進行敏感性分析，以確定哪些缺陷對進度影響最大

Your answer is incorrect

Root cause analysis to identify the underlying causes of all defect types
