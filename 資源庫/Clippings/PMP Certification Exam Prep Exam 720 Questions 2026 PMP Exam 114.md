Question 135:問題135：

A project manager, experienced in utilizing a mix of agile and traditional methodologies, is tasked with rescuing a project that has deviated significantly from its budget and timeline. As the project manager is getting up to speed, additional delays occur, leading to dissatisfaction among stakeholders who then request the senior management for a change in project leadership.  
一位專案經理，擅長運用敏捷與傳統方法，負責拯救一個大幅偏離預算與時程的專案。隨著專案經理逐漸熟悉，仍會發生更多延誤，導致利害關係人不滿，進而要求高層管理更換專案領導。

What action should the project manager take in response to stakeholder dissatisfaction?  
專案經理應如何回應利害關係人的不滿？

Your answer is incorrect 你的回答不正確

Implement a structured feedback mechanism for continuous stakeholder engagement and project alignment  
實施結構化的回饋機制，以持續促進利害關係人參與與專案對齊

Correct answer 正確答案

Report the concerns raised by stakeholders to the higher management and seek guidance on addressing the discord  
向高層管理反映利害關係人提出的疑慮，並尋求解決分歧的指導

Launch a stakeholder satisfaction survey to quantify concerns and prioritize action items  
啟動利害關係人滿意度調查，量化關切並優先排序行動項目

Focus on implementing quick wins to demonstrate progress and reassess project priorities with key stakeholders  
專注於實施快速勝利，以展示進展並與關鍵利害關係人重新評估專案優先順序
