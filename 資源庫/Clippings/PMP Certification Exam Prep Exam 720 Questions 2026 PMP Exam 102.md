Question 105:

In a scenario where a project manager leading a large-scale software integration project is dealing with repeated delays and bugs from their current software vendor, the decision has been made to terminate the contract and engage a new, innovative vendor from a country with a significantly different business culture.  
在領導大型軟體整合專案的專案經理面臨現有軟體供應商反覆的延遲與錯誤時，決定終止合約，並聘用來自一個商業文化截然不同的國家的新穎創新供應商。

What is the project manager's first action to facilitate this vendor change?  
專案經理為了促成這次供應商變更，首先會採取什麼行動？

Correct answer 正確答案

Coordinate with the legal team to understand the implications of the contract termination  
與法律團隊協調，了解合約終止的影響

Initiate a risk assessment to evaluate the impact of transitioning to a vendor in a different legal jurisdiction  
啟動風險評估，評估轉換至不同法律管轄區供應商的影響

Consult with industry peers for insights into managing international software vendor relationships  
請諮詢產業同儕，獲取管理國際軟體供應商關係的見解

Your answer is incorrect 你的回答不正確

Investigate international trade regulations that may affect the procurement of software services from the new vendor  
調查可能影響新供應商軟體服務採購的國際貿易法規
