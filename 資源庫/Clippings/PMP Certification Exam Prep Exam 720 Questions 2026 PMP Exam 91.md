Question 96:

During a particularly challenging period in a major software development project, the project manager notices increasing signs of stress among the team members, which could potentially derail project progress.  
在一個重大軟體開發專案中特別艱難的時期，專案經理注意到團隊成員壓力增加，這可能會阻礙專案進展。

What should the project manager do?  
專案經理應該怎麼做？

Implement regular team-building activities to strengthen camaraderie and alleviate stress  
定期舉辦團隊建立活動，以加強團隊情誼並減輕壓力

Correct answer 正確答案

Foster a respectful and equitable work environment to enhance team performance under pressure  
營造尊重且公平的工作環境，以提升團隊在壓力下的表現

Your answer is incorrect 你的回答不正確

Initiate a peer-support program where team members can share concerns and solutions  
啟動同儕支持計畫，讓團隊成員能分享關切與解決方案

Arrange stress management training to equip the team with strategies to handle project pressures  
安排壓力管理訓練，讓團隊掌握應對專案壓力的策略
