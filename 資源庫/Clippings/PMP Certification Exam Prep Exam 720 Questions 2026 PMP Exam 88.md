Question 90:

As the deadline for a technology rollout project looms, the project manager realizes that the buffer period has been fully utilized. Implementing stringent controls to meet the deadline has led to unease among the project team.

What strategy should the project manager employ to alleviate team concerns while ensuring the project's timely completion?  
專案經理應該採取什麼策略來緩解團隊的疑慮，同時確保專案準時完成？

Clarify the criticality of each task to the team to underscore the necessity of the stringent controls  
向團隊說明每項任務的重要性，強調嚴格管控的必要性

Simplify the project workflow to reduce complexity and potentially accelerate task completion  
簡化專案工作流程，降低複雜度並可能加速任務完成

Your answer is incorrect 你的回答不正確

Introduce flexible work hours to allow the team to manage their time more effectively while meeting project deadlines

Correct answer 正確答案
