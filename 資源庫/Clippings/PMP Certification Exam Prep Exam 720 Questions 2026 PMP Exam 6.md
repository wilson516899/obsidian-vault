Question 17:

A dynamic new project manager has joined an agile project for launching a new online banking system. The newly appointed product owner proposes some innovative features that don't align with the financial institution's strategic objectives as laid out by the Chief Financial Officer (CFO). The product owner, not fully aware of the strategic mismatch, believes the CFO's approval isn't necessary at this stage.  
一位充滿活力的新項目經理加入了敏捷項目，該項目旨在推出新的在線銀行系統。這位新任命的產品負責人，提出了一些與首席財務官（CFO）設定的戰略目標不一致的創新功能。這位產品負責人，可能沒有充分意識到這種戰略上的不一致，因此認為目前不需要獲得 CFO 的批准。

What should the project manager do?  
項目經理應該做什麼？

Arrange a formal presentation for the product owner to explain the project's alignment with the CFO's strategy  
組織一次正式的演示，讓產品負責人向財務總監解釋該項目的戰略與財務總監戰略的契合點。

Correct answer 正確答案

Encourage the product owner to include the CFO in strategic discussions  
建議產品負責人將財務總監納入戰略討論中。

Your answer is incorrect 您的回答不正確。

Initiate a strategic review session with project stakeholders to discuss feature implications  
組織一次戰略性審查會議，與項目相關方討論功能的影響

Update the project documentation to reflect discussions about strategic alignment  
更新項目文檔，以反映關於戰略一致性的討論。
