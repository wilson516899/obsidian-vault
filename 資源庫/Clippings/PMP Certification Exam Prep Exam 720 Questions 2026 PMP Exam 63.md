Question 37:問題37：

Facing a project with an immovable deadline, the team identifies an overly ambitious requirements list that threatens timely completion.  
面對一個無法改變的期限專案，團隊發現一份過於雄心勃勃的需求清單，威脅到及時完成的難度。

What should the project manager do first to ensure the project's success?  
專案經理應該先做什麼，才能確保專案成功？

Your answer is incorrect 你的回答不正確

Document the overabundance of requirements as a risk and outline alternative strategies to maintain the schedule.  
記錄過多需求作為風險，並列出維持時程的替代策略。

Negotiate with the client to prioritize the project backlog for feasible delivery within the established timeframe.  
與客戶協商，優先處理專案積壓事項，以確保在既定時間內可行交付。

Expand the team's size to accelerate the project's progress and meet the project deadline.  
擴大團隊規模，加速專案進度並趕上專案期限。

Correct answer 正確答案

Encourage the team to define a minimum viable product (MVP) that satisfies the most critical project goals by the deadline.  
鼓勵團隊在截止日前定義一個能滿足最關鍵專案目標的最小可行產品（MVP）。
