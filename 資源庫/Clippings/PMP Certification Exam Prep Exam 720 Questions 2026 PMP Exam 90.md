Discovering that one project team member feels their efforts are undervalued compared to others, leading to a decline in their participation and the overall team spirit, what should the project manager do to mend the situation and uplift team cohesion?  
發現有專案團隊成員覺得自己的努力被低估，導致參與度下降，整體團隊精神也因此下降，專案經理應該怎麼做才能改善這種情況並提升團隊凝聚力？

Schedule individual performance reviews to provide personalized feedback and growth plans  
安排個別績效評估，提供個人化回饋與成長計畫

強調專案的集體成就，強調成功應歸功於整個團隊，而非個人成就
