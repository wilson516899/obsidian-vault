Question 19:

During a daily stand-up, a team member from an agile project focused on software for automated manufacturing systems reports that the software's critical third-party library will soon be out of support, and transitioning to a new version would incur considerable costs.  
在每日例行會議上，一個參與敏捷項目的團隊成員報告稱，他們的項目使用的關鍵第三方庫即將停止維護，而升級到新版本將產生相當大的成本。

What should the project manager do next?  
項目經理接下來應該怎麼做？

Correct answer 正確答案

Analyze the feasibility and impact of upgrading to the new library version  
分析升級到新版本圖書館的可行性和影響

Consult with the financial department to evaluate the budgetary implications of the upgrade  
諮詢財務部門，以評估升級方案的財務影響

Investigate alternative third-party libraries that could replace the current one  
調查可以替代現有庫的第三方庫

Your answer is incorrect 您的回答不正確。

Review the project timeline to assess the potential delays caused by the library transition  
審查項目時間表，以評估圖書館遷移可能造成的延誤。
