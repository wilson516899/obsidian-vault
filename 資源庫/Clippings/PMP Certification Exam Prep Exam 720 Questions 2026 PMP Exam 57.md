Question 10:

Case Study 2: Q4

A project manager has been assigned to lead a twelve-month initiative to expand a regional hospital network's patient records system to three newly acquired community clinics. The project is sponsored by the Chief Information Officer.

During planning, the project manager identified key stakeholders including the CIO, the IT department, clinic administrators, and nursing staff representatives. The project manager documented each stakeholder group's communication preferences, the frequency and format of updates they would receive, and the methods that would be used to distribute information throughout the project. Monthly status reports were planned for the CIO, weekly emails for the IT department, and quarterly briefings for clinic administrators. Nursing staff were designated to receive the same monthly status reports as the CIO.

Six months into the project, nursing staff at the first clinic began raising concerns about the new system's impact on their daily workflows. They found the new interface significantly more time-consuming than their previous system and complained that they had not been consulted during the design phase. The nursing staff escalated their concerns to the clinic administrator, who contacted the CIO directly.

The CIO called an emergency meeting with the project manager, expressing frustration that this issue had not been surfaced earlier. The project manager explained that nursing staff had been included in the distribution of monthly status reports. The CIO pointed out that the status reports were written in technical language and contained no information relevant to clinical workflows.

Meanwhile the project manager learned that the hospital network's Chief Medical Officer had significant concerns about patient data privacy and regulatory compliance implications of the system expansion. The CMO had been informally voicing these concerns to clinic administrators for weeks but had never been contacted by the project manager. Two clinics remain to be integrated and the project timeline is at risk.  
同時，專案經理得知醫院網路的首席醫療長對系統擴展的病患資料隱私及法規合規影響抱有重大疑慮。首席醫療官已非正式地向診所管理者表達這些擔憂數週，但從未收到專案經理的聯繫。還有兩家診所尚未整合，且專案時程面臨風險。

Question 4: The nursing staff have raised concerns about the new system's impact on their daily workflows. What should the project manager do?  
問題四：護理人員對新系統對日常工作流程的影響表示擔憂。專案經理應該怎麼做？

Your answer is incorrect 你的回答不正確

Send a revised status report to the nursing staff explaining the system changes and their benefits.  
向護理人員發送修訂後的狀態報告，說明系統變更及其好處。

Escalate the nursing staff's concerns to the CIO and request guidance on how to proceed.  
將護理人員的疑慮升級到 CIO，並請求如何處理的指導。

Correct answer 正確答案

Meet directly with the nursing staff to understand their workflow concerns before proceeding with the remaining clinic integrations.  
在進行剩餘的診所整合前，請直接與護理人員會面，了解他們的工作流程問題。

Proceed with the remaining clinic integrations and address the nursing staff's workflow concerns post-launch.  
繼續進行剩餘的診所整合，並在上線後解決護理人員的工作流程問題。
