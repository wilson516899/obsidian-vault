Question 77:問題77：

In the midst of a software development project, the project manager notices that the software's features are not aligning with the client's requirements, leading to an increase in bugs reported during testing. This misalignment has caused the project's schedule to slip, moving the schedule performance index (SPI) from 1.05 to 0.85 and the cost performance index (CPI) from 1.2 to 0.95.  
在軟體開發專案進行中，專案經理發現軟體功能與客戶需求不符，導致測試中回報的錯誤增加。這種錯位導致專案進度延遲，計畫績效指數（SPI）從 1.05 降至 0.85，成本績效指數（CPI）從 1.2 降至 0.95。

What is the most effective course of action for the project manager to ensure that the project meets its deadlines and quality requirements in future phases?  
專案經理在未來階段確保專案能達成期限與品質要求，最有效的行動方案是什麼？

Correct answer 正確答案

Implement a code review process to identify and address defects before they progress to the testing phase  
實施程式碼審查流程，在缺陷進入測試階段前識別並修正

Enhance communication with stakeholders to refine project requirements and expectations  
加強與利害關係人的溝通，以精煉專案需求與期望

Introduce automated testing tools to increase efficiency and coverage of bug detection  
引入自動化測試工具，提升錯誤偵測的效率與覆蓋範圍

Your answer is incorrect 你的回答不正確

Revisit and adjust the project scope to better align with current project realities and client needs  
重新檢視並調整專案範圍，以更符合當前專案的實際情況與客戶需求
