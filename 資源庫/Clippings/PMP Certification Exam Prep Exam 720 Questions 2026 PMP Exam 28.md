Question 62:

An organization is evaluating different project management approaches and wants to understand what distinguishes Agile from traditional methodologies. The leadership team asks the project management office to explain the fundamental nature of Agile.  
某組織正在評估不同的項目管理方式，並希望瞭解敏捷式管理與傳統管理方法的差異所在。該組織的領導層要求項目管理部門說明敏捷式管理的本質。

What is Agile?什麼是敏捷開發？

A specific project management framework with defined roles and ceremonies  
一個具有明確角色與流程的專項項目管理架構

A software development methodology created by a single company  
由單一家公司所創造的軟體開發方法論

Correct answer 正確答案

A mindset and set of values focused on iterative delivery and customer collaboration  
一種以重複性交付和與客戶合作為核心的思維方式與價值觀。

Your answer is incorrect 您的回答不正確。

A scheduling technique for managing complex projects with uncertain requirements  
一種用於管理需求不確定的複雜專案的排程技術
