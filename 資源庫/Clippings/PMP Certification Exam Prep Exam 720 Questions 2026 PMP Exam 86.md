Question 85:問題85：

On a large-scale infrastructure project, the project manager identifies that the materials supplied by a key vendor are not meeting the specified quality standards, risking the project's structural integrity and safety. The project is already tightly scheduled and any delays in resolving this issue could lead to significant setbacks.  
在大型基礎建設專案中，專案經理發現關鍵供應商供應的材料未達指定品質標準，導致專案結構完整性與安全受到威脅。該項目已經排程緊湊，任何延遲解決此問題都可能導致重大挫折。

What is the most immediate action the project manager should take to address the quality issue without causing project delays?  
專案經理應該採取最立即的行動來解決品質問題，同時避免造成專案延誤？

Correct answer 正確答案

Initiate a quality audit of the supplied materials to document discrepancies and provide evidence for discussions with the vendor  
啟動對所提供材料的品質稽核，以記錄差異並提供證據，供與供應商討論

Your answer is incorrect 你的回答不正確

Replace the vendor immediately with another supplier known for higher quality materials, despite potential cost increases  
立即以其他以高品質材料聞名的供應商取代該供應商，儘管成本可能增加

Negotiate with the current vendor for a rush delivery of higher quality materials as a replacement  
與現有供應商協商，要求以更高品質的材料加急交付作為替代品

Request the project team to adjust the design specifications to accommodate the available material quality  
請專案團隊調整設計規格，以符合現有材料品質
