Question 60:問題60：

After wrapping up the design phase of a new product development project, the project manager shares that the project is on track regarding time, budget, and specified design features. However, the lead designer expresses concern that the project has not yet addressed potential market competitiveness and customer experience improvements.  
在完成新產品開發專案設計階段後，專案經理會分享專案在時間、預算及指定設計特徵方面均按計畫進行。然而，首席設計師表達了對專案尚未解決潛在市場競爭力及客戶體驗改善的擔憂。

What could be the underlying issue?  
可能的根本問題是什麼？

Your answer is incorrect 你的回答不正確

The project team focused too narrowly on technical specifications without considering market trends.  
專案團隊過於專注於技術規格，卻忽略了市場趨勢。

Key stakeholder feedback was not adequately sought or integrated into the project planning process.  
關鍵利害關係人的回饋未被充分徵求或整合進專案規劃過程。

Correct answer 正確答案

The benefits realization plan was not incorporated into the project plan.  
效益實現計畫未納入專案計畫中。
