Question 21:

A project manager is leading an agile project for developing a new cloud-based CRM system amidst a rapidly evolving market landscape. The project sponsor expresses concerns about staying relevant and ensuring the project delivers the intended business benefits.  
一位項目經理正在領導一個敏捷項目，該項目旨在開發一種新的基於雲的 CRM 系統，而該項目正處於快速變化的市場環境中。項目贊助方對保持相關性以及確保項目能夠實現預期的商業效益表示擔憂。

What should the project manager do to adapt to market changes effectively?  
項目經理應該如何有效地適應市場變化？

Your answer is incorrect 您的回答不正確。

Conduct frequent stakeholder meetings to reassess project objectives and requirements  
定期與各方進行溝通，以重新評估項目目標和需求。

Halt the project immediately if a significant market change undermines the product’s relevance  
如果市場出現重大變化，導致產品不再具有市場價值，應立即停止該項目。

Correct answer 正確答案

Regularly review and adjust the product backlog to reflect changing business needs  
定期審查和調整產品待辦事項，以適應不斷變化的業務需求。

Use continuous integration practices to allow for immediate adjustments in development  
採用持續集成方法，以便在開發過程中進行即時調整。
