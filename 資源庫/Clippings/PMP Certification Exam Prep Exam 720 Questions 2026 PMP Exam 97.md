Question 100:問題100：

Stakeholders were surprised by equipment procurement that didn’t match their current expectations due to changes in initial requirements not being clearly communicated.  
利害關係人對於設備採購結果與他們目前預期不符感到驚訝，原因是初期需求變動未被清楚傳達。

What should the project manager have done?  
專案經理應該怎麼做？

Made sure that project requirements were validated and shared with the procurement team.  
確保專案需求被驗證並與採購團隊分享。

Your answer is incorrect 你的回答不正確

Ensured the evolving needs and requests of stakeholders were closely tracked.  
確保利害關係人不斷變化的需求與請求被密切追蹤。

Made sure procurement contracts could be canceled if needed under such circumstances.  
確保採購合約在這種情況下可以取消。

Correct answer 正確答案

Ensured that all stakeholders were informed about changes to the requirements as they occurred.  
確保所有利害關係人都能在需求變更發生時獲得通知。
