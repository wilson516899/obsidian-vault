Question 61:問題61：

During a discussion with team members, the project manager learns that the most recent retrospective identified obsolete equipment as a potential risk to the upcoming iteration. The team recommends purchasing new equipment, stating it is essential for successful delivery of the final product.  
在與團隊成員的討論中，專案經理得知最新的回顧報告指出過時設備是對即將到來迭代的潛在風險。團隊建議購買新設備，認為這對最終產品順利交付至關重要。

What should the project manager do next?  
專案經理接下來該做什麼？

Check the project budget to verify available contingency reserve  
請查閱專案預算以確認可用的應急儲備

Correct answer 正確答案

Review the cost management plan to determine how to address this issue  
檢視成本管理計畫，決定如何解決此問題

Inform the team that additional equipment falls outside the project scope  
告知團隊額外的設備不屬於專案範圍

Your answer is incorrect 你的回答不正確

Submit a change request to increase the budget and purchase new equipment  
提交變更申請以增加預算並購買新設備
