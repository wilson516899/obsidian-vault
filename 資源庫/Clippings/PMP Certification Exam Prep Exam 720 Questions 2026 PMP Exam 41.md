Your answer is incorrect 您的回答不正確。

Project manager 項目經理

In Extreme Programming, the coach (sometimes called XP coach or iteration manager) is responsible for facilitating team meetings, ceremonies, and processes. The coach ensures the team follows XP practices correctly, facilitates standup meetings and planning sessions, helps remove impediments, and coaches team members on XP principles and practices. This role is similar to the Scrum Master in Scrum but specifically focused on XP methodology. The coach serves as a servant leader who guides the team's process adherence without managing individual work or making technical decisions.  
在極限編程（XP）中，教練（有時也被稱為 XP 導師或迭代經理）負責組織團隊會議、儀式和流程。教練確保團隊正確地遵循 XP 的實踐，主持站立會議和計劃會議，幫助解決團隊遇到的問題，並指導團隊成員瞭解 XP 的原則和實踐。這個角色類似於 Scrum 中的 Scrum Master，但專注於 XP 方法。教練扮演著一種服務型領導者的角色，引導團隊按照流程進行，而無需管理個人工作或做出技術決策。
